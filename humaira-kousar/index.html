Frequently Asked Questions

What does a Solution Architect do?
A Solution Architect maps out complete tech solutions that match business aims. They connect what the business needs with how it's built technically, covering scalability, speed, security, and upkeep.
I want to build a scalable e-commerce platform. Can you help?
Absolutely. We've handled scalable e-commerce builds before, from planning the structure and choosing tech to fine-tuning performance.
Does Humaira have references or experience working with clients in the USA?
Yes, Humara's teamed up with clients worldwide, USA included, and happy to share references when needed.
My software is unstable. Can Humaira identify and resolve the issues?
Sure thing. Instability often stems from things like bad architecture, sloppy code, messy processes, green teams, shifting requirements, tricky legacy ties, or wrong tech choices. A thorough check and fix plan can steady things up and boost performance.
I am a founder with strong business knowledge but limited technical expertise. Can I get support?
Absolutely. We love partnering with business-savvy founders, turning your ideas into solid tech and walking you through the choices.
What is the difference between a Solution Architect and a Technical Architect?
Solution Architect: Looks at the big picture across systems and ties it to business goals.

Technical Architect: Dives into particular tech, tools or parts of the build.
Can Humaira help in standardizing and improving security practices?
Yes. We'll review your setup, spot weak points, and roll out top practices for compliance, data safety, and solid protection.

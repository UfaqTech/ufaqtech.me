Engineering Capabilities

Our Core Industrial Automation Services

PLC programming and design of PLC control system

In any automation-based manufacturing system, Programmable Logic Controllers (PLCs) are at the heart of the system. We program, design, and commission PLC based control systems to increase machine reliability and enhance process consistency. We support all major PLC platforms and offer complete documentation, ladder logic development and structured text programming for easy maintenance and expansion of your systems.

Being a full-service industrial automation company in Lahore, our PLC programming services include new installations, system upgrade, retrofitting of obsolete controllers and migrating the production system from relay logic to modern programmable controller, reducing downtimes during the migration.

SCADA and HMI Development

Operators have the ability to have real-time visibility, control and understanding of plant processes through a supervisory control and data acquisition (SCADA) system and a human-machine interface (HMI). UfaqTech's scalable, secure and user-friendly SCADA and HMI centralize monitoring, create intelligent alarms and deliver historical trend analysis for data-driven decision making.

Our SCADA can integrate with an existing plant infrastructure and have remote monitoring features, allowing plant managers to monitor the plant remotely. We work to provide easy to use interface design to allow operators of all skill levels to interact with the system with confidence.

Design and Fabrication of Electrical Panel

The key of any automation system is a well-engineered control panel. The in-house panel design and fabrication facility of UfaqTech is capable of manufacturing industry-compliant Motor Control Centres (MCC), Power Control Centres (PCC), PLC panels and VFD panels. Each panel is designed based on IS/IEC standards and undergoes high-quality checks before shipment.

Our panel building services are available for 3D layout design, wiring, testing and factory acceptance testing (FAT) so that your panels are delivered ready for site use.

Robotics and Robotic Process Integration

Industrial robotics is changing the productivity of production and UfaqTech is helping businesses to introduce robotic arms and automated material handling systems into production lines. Whether you need a pick and place machine, a welding robot, a palletizing robot, or an assembly automation solution, we can create a robotic cell that boosts production and decreases the need for manual tasks and on-site risks.

Additionally, we can program your robot, design safety fencing and integrate it into current PLC/SCADA systems for easy integration in your automation environment.

VFD and Motor Control

Variable Frequency Drives (VFDs) are vital to energy efficiency and accurate motor control. Our engineers are able to design and implement motor control systems with VFDs which can lower your energy consumption, increase the life of your equipment, and increase the accuracy of your process — which is especially beneficial for pumps, fans, conveyors, and compressors.

Retrofitting and Automation Upgrades

We can help you extend the life of your equipment, make it safer and compliant with current safety regulations and standards and upgrade it to modern automation standards and requirements – without the cost of replacing the entire machine.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What does an industrial automation company do?

Industrial automation company designs, builds and implements industrial automation systems, including PLCs, SCADA, HMI, robotics, sensors and others, to automate manufacturing and process operations, while minimising manual effort and increasing efficiency, safety and consistency.

Why do I need to opt for a local industrial automation company in Lahore while there are other national industrial automation companies available?

A Lahore automation partner such as UfaqTech provides quicker response time on-site, knowledge of the local industry requirement and ease of coordination for installing, training and maintenance.

What's the price of industrial automation for a small manufacturing unit?

The cost ranges from machine to machine; for example, an upgrade to PLCs on one machine will cost much less than a full production line automation project. After assessing your process needs and objectives, UfaqTech will quote you a customised price.

What is the difference between PLC and SCADA system?

The hardware controller that runs the automation logic on the machines is known as a PLC, and SCADA is the software layer that monitors, visualises, and manages data from several PLCs in real time in a plant.

Is there any possibility for taking old machinery to an automated state or should it be replaced?

A large percentage of the existing machinery can be replaced by modern PLCs, drives, and sensors without having to replace the whole machine, thereby giving a longer life to the equipment and enhancing its performance at a reduced cost.

What is an average industrial automation project cost?

The length of time for timelines may vary depending on the complexity of the project, for example, a simple panel upgrade might take a few weeks to design, commission and train, whereas complete plant automation projects may take several months.

The top industries that gain the most from industrial automation are?

Individually, industries such as automotive, pharmaceuticals, food and beverage, packaging, chemicals, electronics, textile manufacturing, and others benefit from the highest ROI from industrial automation because the manufacturing processes are high-volume, quality-driven.

What is the fourth industrial revolution and what does it have to do with industrial automation?

Industry 4.0 is the use of connected devices, data analytics and the Internet of Things (IoT) in existing automation systems and enables predictive maintenance, remote monitoring, and data-driven decision-making throughout the manufacturing process.

Do industrial automation companies offer post-sales support?

You can get Annual Maintenance Contracts (AMC), remote troubleshooting, spare parts support and periodic system health checks from reputable providers such as UfaqTech to guarantee long-term reliability of automation systems.

What are the system's challenges when implementing an automated control system?

If you're plagued by costly staff, poor product quality, frequent equipment downtime, or scalability issues, then your enterprise is probably already prepared for the advantages of automation. Talking with an experienced industrial automation company will be able to gauge feasibility and potential ROI.

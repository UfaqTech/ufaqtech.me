Risk Mitigation

Common Digital Marketing Mistakes We Help Businesses Avoid

Many businesses run into the same avoidable problems that reduce the return on their digital marketing investment. As an experienced digital marketing companies in Lahore, we help clients steer clear of:

Running campaigns without proper tracking

If conversion tracking is not set up correctly from the start, there is no reliable way to know what the campaign is actually producing.

Treating SEO and paid as separate strategies

The two inform each other and businesses that run them independently rather than as a coordinated effort consistently underperform those that do not.

Producing content without a distribution strategy

Content that does not have a plan for reaching the audience it was written for rarely generates the return that justifies the time invested in creating it.

Ignoring the landing page

Driving paid traffic to a homepage or a poorly designed landing page is one of the most reliable ways to waste a PPC budget.

Optimizing for vanity metrics

Follower counts, impressions and click-through rates matter less than the number of qualified leads or conversions the activity is producing.
